# Abna'a AL-Sharqia Dessert & Sweets — Digital Marketing Strategy & Campaigns

> A comprehensive digital marketing project covering market research, brand analysis, strategic planning, media execution, and content development for **Abna'a AL-Sharqia**.

---

## 📌 Project Overview & Idea

* **Project Name:** Abna'a AL-Sharqia Digital Marketing Strategy & Campaign
* **Track:** Digital Marketing – DEPI (Digital Egypt Pioneers Initiative)
* **Instructor / Mentor:** Eng. / Dr. Noura Farrage
* **Project Idea:**  
  This project delivers an end-to-end digital marketing strategy tailored for **Abna'a AL-Sharqia Dessert & Sweets**. The project encompasses in-depth market and competitor research, multi-dimensional business analysis, brand positioning, audience segmentation, and the design of creative marketing campaigns. It establishes a multi-channel content calendar alongside paid acquisition blueprints designed to enhance brand recall, engage local dessert lovers, and drive measurable retail and online conversion growth.

## 🗓️ Work Plan & Execution Phases

* **Phase 1: Research & Discovery**
  * Market scanning, local sweet market segmentation, and buyer persona mapping.
  * Competitor analysis (strengths, weaknesses, offerings, and promotional tactics).
* **Phase 2: Business & Marketing Strategy**
  * Positioning strategy and core value proposition alignment.
  * Selecting marketing channel mix and establishing quarterly campaign roadmaps.
* **Phase 3: Creative Production & Paid Media Framework**
  * Developing content themes, hooks, captions, and visual guidelines.
  * Formulating paid media campaign structures across Meta platforms.
* **Phase 4: Evaluation, Metrics & Handover**
  * Defining performance thresholds and reporting frameworks.
  * Compiling project documentation and deliverable assets.

---

## 🎯 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

* **Brand Awareness & Reach:**
  * Total Targeted Impressions and Unique Reach within target geographic zones.
  * Cost Per Mille (CPM) efficiency benchmarks.
* **Engagement & Consideration:**
  * Engagement Rate (ER) across social channels (Shares, Comments, Saves).
  * Click-Through Rate (CTR) on paid promotional ad sets.
* **Conversion & Foot Traffic:**
  * Inbound inquiries, direct messages (DM volume), and branch visit attribution.
  * Cost Per Lead / Cost Per Conversation (CPL / CPC).
* **Return & Brand Health:**
  * Return on Ad Spend (ROAS) estimation and brand sentiment tracking.
